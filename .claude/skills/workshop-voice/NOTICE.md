## What was changed

- Condensed for this repository, and scoped to workshop materials rather than general drafts.
- The original's detect-only mode and its separate `eval.md` checklist were not carried over.
- **One rule is deliberately overridden.** The original lists "emoji in headings" under formatting slop. This repository uses an emoji on every section heading as a house convention, so that rule does not apply here. Every other pattern in the original list is kept.
